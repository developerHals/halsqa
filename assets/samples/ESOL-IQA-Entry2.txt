<div class="card">
<h4>1 — Content <span style="font-weight:normal; color:#555">(Very Important)</span></h4>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>Minimum 60 words achieved</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Covers task bullet — Description (e.g. appearance, person, place)</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Covers task bullet — Opinion (like/dislike)</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Covers task bullet — Information (what/who/where)</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Covers task bullet — Question (email task)</label></li></ul>

<h4>2 — Structure</h4>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>At least 5–6 sentences written (to reach 60-word standard)</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Logical order: beginning → middle → end</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Basic cohesion used: <em>and / because / then</em></label></li></ul>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h4>3 — Grammar</h4>
<span class="subh">AC 1.1 — Sentence Formation</span>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>At least <strong>2</strong> correctly formed sentences (subject + verb + clear meaning)</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Uses simple present or past tense appropriately</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Tense is mostly consistent — no unexplained shifts</label></li></ul>
<span class="subh">AC 1.2 — Conjunctions</span>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>Uses at least <strong>2</strong> basic conjunctions:
<em>and / but / because / so / or</em></label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Conjunctions used correctly to join ideas or sentences</label></li></ul>
<div class="green-box"><strong>Grammar Pass Rule (AC 1.1 &amp; 1.2):</strong><br>
✓ Minimum 2 correctly formed sentences<br>
✓ Minimum 2 conjunctions used correctly </div>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h4>4 — Vocabulary</h4>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>At least 2 descriptive adjectives (e.g. <em>&quot;friendly teacher&quot;, &quot;nice holiday&quot;</em>)</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Some topic-specific vocabulary used appropriately</label></li></ul>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h4>5 — Punctuation</h4>
<span class="subh">AC 1.4 — Punctuation</span>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>Capital letters used correctly at sentence starts</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Full stops used correctly at sentence ends</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Sentences clearly separated (not run-on)</label></li></ul>
<div class="green-box"><strong>Minimum (AC 1.4):</strong> At least <strong>2</strong> sentences correctly punctuated with capital letter and full stop.</div>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h4>6 — Spelling</h4>
<ul class="cl">
<li><input type="checkbox"><label>Most common words spelled correctly</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Phonetic spelling acceptable where meaning remains clear</label></li><li><input type="checkbox"><label>Errors do not block understanding</label></li></ul>
</div>
<div class="green-box" style="margin-top:14px"><strong>Overall Pass Rule — Tasks 2 &amp; 3:</strong><br>
✓ Length achieved (60+ words)<br>
✓ All content points covered<br>
✓ At least 2 correctly formed sentences (AC 1.1)<br>
✓ At least 2 conjunctions used correctly (AC 1.2)<br>
✓ At least 2 sentences correctly punctuated (AC 1.4)<br>
✓ Evidence of independence (not copied or over-scaffolded) </div>
